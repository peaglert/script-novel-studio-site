Refund Policy

The simple version

If you change your mind within 14 days of a charge and you have not used any of the credits that came with it, we will refund it in full. If any of those credits have been used, that purchase cannot be refunded. We do not give partial refunds.

Subscriptions

You can cancel a subscription at any time. Your plan stays active until the end of the period you paid for, and it does not renew after that. For a refund, contact us within 14 days of the charge, and the credits for that period must be completely unused. A refunded charge ends the access that came with it. Your own project files stay on your computer either way.

Credit packs and the lifetime license

The same rule applies. Contact us within 14 days of purchase, and the credits from that purchase must be completely unused.

How to ask

Email us at support@scriptnovelpublishing.com with the email address on your account and the date of the charge. Approved refunds are returned to the original payment method through Stripe.
