The Partner Program

Partner with Script Novel Studio

Script Novel Studio is a desktop writing app that helps writers finish books: novels, script novels, and screenplays, in English and Spanish. Most writing tools stop at ideas and outlines. Studio can draft an entire book with the writer, who approves every word, and the writer's files stay on their own computer.

Studio grows through people who already serve writers. If that is you, we would like to pay you for telling them about it.

Payouts

How you get paid

Monthly payouts, $50 minimum

Payouts go out monthly. If a month comes in under $50, your balance rolls forward until it clears. Nothing is lost.

An itemized statement with every payout

Every payout comes with a statement showing the date, the product, the amount, your commission, and the status of each sale, so you can check every dollar.

Refunds are shown, never hidden

A sale becomes payable after the 14-day refund line. If a sale is refunded, it shows on your statement as reversed. And we never share your buyers' names or emails with anyone.

The short version

House rules

  • Disclose your link. Always mark your partner link with a plain note or a simple #ad.
  • Promote honestly. Only claim what this website claims.
  • No spam. Ever.
  • No bidding on our name. Do not bid on the words Script Novel Studio in ads.
  • No commissions on your own purchases.

We may end a partnership that breaks these rules. Commissions already earned are always paid.

How to apply

Tell us who you are

Email us with four things: your name; your website, channel, or show; what your audience is and roughly how big it is; and where you would share your link.

A real person reads every application. If it is a fit, we set up your link and send your partner packet.

Email your application or write to us at publish@scriptnovelpublishing.com

Program terms may change for future sales, with notice. Commissions already earned are always honored. Full partner terms come with the partner packet.
