Write with AI

Ask for the task, not the agent

No agent names to decode. One gold button in the editor opens a menu in plain author language, and before anything runs, the studio tells you what it will read and where the result will land.

The Write with AI menu

Seven choices, written the way authors talk about their work:

  • Write my first paragraph, built from your synopsis
  • Continue writing from your cursor, or Improve my writing
  • Get feedback on my story: plot, pacing, and continuity reports
  • Ask a question about your book or the craft
  • Write this chapter, or Write entire book with the credit estimate shown first

A synopsis and outline your agents actually read

Your synopsis lives in the open, in the Outline tab, where you can see it and keep it current. When a chapter is empty, the agents work from your outline idea first, then your outline, then your synopsis, and every result names the basis it used. No more answers about the wrong story.

Build the outline from your idea with one click, expand it into a beat for every chapter, and set a target word count so chapter drafts are written toward the length of the book you mean to finish.

Chapter by chapter, and nothing lands unreviewed

Ask for a chapter and the draft goes to a review queue, not into your manuscript. Read it there, accept it, send it back with a note to revise, or discard it. Accepting is the only way a draft becomes part of your book.

The queue keeps your drafts when you close the app, and a whole-book run writes every empty chapter in order, skips the ones you have already written, and can be stopped after any chapter.

See the printed book before you print it

Choose a publishing target and the studio applies that platform's rules for you: Amazon KDP Print gutter widths by page count, KDP Kindle, and IngramSpark inside-margin minimums, with a warning past KDP's 828-page cap. Every adjustment is reported, never silent.

The print preview lays out real pages at your trim size with your title page, copyright page, and dedication up front, running heads, and page numbers that start on chapter one.

Good questions

Frequently asked questions

Can I upgrade or downgrade later?

Yes. Upgrades take effect right away: Stripe credits the unused time on your current plan, you pay only the difference for the rest of the period, and your credits top up by the difference between the plans. Downgrades take effect when your current paid period ends, so you keep everything you have already paid for. You will never end up subscribed to two plans at once.

What happens if I cancel?

You keep full access until the end of the period you paid for. After that the studio locks, but your project files live on your own computer and are never changed or deleted. Resubscribe any time and everything is right where you left it. A lifetime license never locks.

How many computers can I use?

Each purchase can be activated on up to 2 computers.

What do credits cover?

Credits meter the AI writing help: drafting, rewriting, reports, and questions. Writing by hand, organizing chapters, and exporting your book are always free, and they work offline.

Does it work in Spanish?

Yes. The whole interface switches between English and Spanish, and with Spanish on, the AI drafts in Spanish too. Your manuscript itself is never translated or changed.

What if I run out of credits?

The AI pauses; your writing and exports keep working. Add a one-time credit pack, or wait for your plan's monthly refill. Plan credits refresh in full each period, and unused plan credits do not roll over. Credits you buy in a pack are different: they stay yours until you use them, and they never expire.
