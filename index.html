Good questions

Frequently asked questions

Can I upgrade or downgrade later?

Yes. Upgrades take effect right away: Stripe credits the unused time on your current plan, you pay only the difference for the rest of the period, and your credits top up by the difference between the plans. Downgrades take effect when your current paid period ends, so you keep everything you have already paid for. You will never end up subscribed to two plans at once.

What happens if I cancel?

You keep full access until the end of the period you paid for. After that the studio locks, but your project files live on your own computer and are never changed or deleted. Resubscribe any time and everything is right where you left it. A lifetime license never locks.

How many computers can I use?

Each purchase can be activated on up to 2 computers.

What do credits cover?

Credits meter the AI writing help: drafting, rewriting, reports, and questions. Writing by hand, organizing chapters, and exporting your book are always free, and they work offline.

Does it work in Spanish?

Yes. The whole interface switches between English and Spanish, and with Spanish on, the AI drafts in Spanish too. Your manuscript itself is never translated or changed.

What if I run out of credits?

The AI pauses; your writing and exports keep working. Add a one-time credit pack, or wait for your plan's monthly refill. Plan credits refresh in full each period, and unused plan credits do not roll over. Credits you buy in a pack are different: they stay yours until you use them, and they never expire.

Does Script Novel Studio replace a professional editor?

No, and we recommend one. Studio writes with you, and its checks catch a great deal, but before you publish, have your manuscript, screenplay, or script novel edited by a professional editor.

What kind of computer do I need?

Script Novel Studio is a Windows program. It runs on a Windows desktop or laptop, including a Windows tablet like a Surface. There is no Mac, iPhone, iPad, Android, or Chromebook version, and it cannot be installed on those devices.
